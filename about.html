WHO WE ARE

Your Connection to Pakistani Products.

A. Awan Port City Traders (Pvt) Ltd is a Pakistan-based export sourcing and trade coordination company focused on connecting international buyers with product sourcing opportunities in Pakistan.

We work to understand buyer requirements and coordinate with relevant suppliers to discuss product specifications, quantities, packaging, quotations and shipment arrangements.

Our approach is based on clear communication, practical coordination and understanding the requirements of international trade partners.

  • Pakistan-based product sourcing
  • Fresh produce and agricultural products
  • Rice, textiles and other trading categories
  • International buyer and supplier coordination
  • Quotation and shipment requirement discussions
